Why we build Tryll

Games are the most interactive medium people have made: the player doesn’t just watch or listen, they act, and the world is supposed to answer. That’s why we believe AI belongs in games before it belongs anywhere else. We build Tryll as hardcore gamers, for game developers, so the world can finally listen, remember and react to the person holding the controller.
