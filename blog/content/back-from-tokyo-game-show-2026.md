**Tokyo Game Show** wrapped up last week, and [Iryna B.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/iryna-b-1a5478127/) who was there representing Tryll, shared a few thoughts on the event.

Her take on the show:

> "It's a great place to get inspired and see lots of creative ideas in one place. You get to meet really talented people, play their games, share feedback and hopefully help them grow. But more and more games are starting to look the same. There are more copycat projects every year and sometimes it was hard to tell which studio made which game. That's why the new and original ideas really caught the eye."
