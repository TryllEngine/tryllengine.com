Real Talk

Say what you actually want to say. Tryll handles speech-to-text and text-to-speech on the player’s device, with no servers in between. You get voiced NPCs in various languages including Korean, Japanese, without recording every line.

You build the world and set the rules. Tryll creates agents and makes sure they follow them. Players get unscripted interactions and a reliable, immersive experience.
