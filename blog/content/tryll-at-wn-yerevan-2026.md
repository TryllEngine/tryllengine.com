On **October 15-16**, Tryll will be at [WN Events](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wnconf/) **in Yerevan**, represented by one of our co-founders and CBO, [Alex Riabov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alex-riabov1/). If you're interested in the topic, come by and join the discussion.

To learn more about creating **realistic minds for your games** and increasing immersion for your players, ping us in the comments or [reach out](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alex-riabov1/) to Alex directly, and we'll set up a time for a meeting.
