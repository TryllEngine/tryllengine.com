We are back from ChinaJoy. This year's theme was **"Level Up with AI!"**, and it was indeed reflected in the majority of conversations.

The interesting shift was in the questions. Almost nobody was debating *whether* AI belongs in game development. People asked about specific cases: which mechanic it actually powers, what it costs to run at scale, how to make sure that the models are under control before the game ships. So we spent most of the show talking about our solution, which lets developers run **AI on the player's own GPU instead of the cloud**, and the new game mechanics that opens up.
